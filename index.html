nodelings

CONNECT · LOOP · PLAY

A cosy connect-the-nodes puzzle game for iPhone and iPad.

Support

Questions, ideas or a bug to report? Email apps@feingold5.com. You can also tap Send feedback in the app's Settings.

Restoring your unlock

If you've bought Unlock every level and it isn't showing on a new device, open Settings in nodelings and tap Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account you bought it with.

Your progress

Progress, stars, suns and power-ups are saved on your device. To start over, open the level map and tap Reset progress at the bottom.

Learning the game

The animated walkthrough can be replayed any time from Settings, and the Game glossary explains every node, tile, companion and power-up, with live demos.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

nodelings does not collect, store or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no advertising, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party SDKs, and the game doesn't send anything to servers run by us.

What stays on your device

  • Your level progress, stars, suns, power-ups and settings (sound, music, haptics) are saved only on your device.
  • If you use iCloud or computer backups, these files can be included like any other app's data. Those backups are handled by Apple under its own policies.

In-app purchase

The optional one-time purchase that unlocks every level is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. The app only checks with Apple, on your device, whether the unlock has been bought.

Ratings and reviews

Now and then the app may show Apple's standard request to rate the game. Anything you submit goes to Apple, not to us.

Feedback email

If you choose to send feedback, your email app sends it to us. We use your address and message only to read and reply to you, and we never share them.

Children

nodelings is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no personal data, it collects none from children either.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions about privacy: apps@feingold5.com.
